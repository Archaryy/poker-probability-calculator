Poker words, explained
Equity (win chance)
How often you win, counting a tie as half a win, if the rest of the hand is dealt out many times.
Pot odds and break-even
The least you must win for a call to make money. It is your call divided by the pot after you call.
EV (expected value)
Your average profit or loss if you made this exact call over and over. Plus means call, minus means fold.
Outs
Unseen cards that would improve your hand. More outs means a better chance to catch up.
Range
The set of hands an opponent could hold. "Top 10%" means only the strongest starting hands.
Flop, turn, river
The first three shared cards, then the fourth, then the fifth.
